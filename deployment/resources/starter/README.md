# CityChat starter

This is a deliberately small, non-operational StoryCell specimen for playground artifact v0.7.0. It implements the plain-language reading order of approved normative CityChat DS Add-on v0.7; it is not DS Add-on v0.7.0 authority and does not implement persistence, CityScan scoring, LINE, officer operations or sharing.

To use it:

1. Keep the vendored Landometer package beside the same `deployment/` directory structure, or update the two stylesheet paths without changing their bytes.
2. Resolve `build-card.yml` for the real route and deployment.
3. Read approved CityChat DS Add-on v0.7 and replace the conceptual fixture with a governed object, evidence and current capability truth.
4. Add an action only when its destination/effect, permission, persistence and recovery exist.
5. Keep controls on the inherited LDS `.btn` or `.btn-icon` contract. Do not replace their geometry, padding, focus, disabled, busy, or press feedback with local values.
6. Before adding a functional icon, resolve its role in `../citychat-ds-addon/v0.7.0/citychat-icon-map.json` and its actual-control binding in `citychat-icon-resolution.json`. A gallery specimen does not enable a capability.
7. Resolve colour jobs through `citychat-color-role-map.json`; follow the complete upstream LDS atlas for shared values and the owning data contract for analytical scales.
8. Resolve motion through `semantic-motion.citychat.yml`. Use the named inherited primitive or `none`; keep the final meaning readable with reduced motion and without JavaScript.
9. Use the exact CityChat lockup only when the active identity manifest approves the build, URL, role, theme, backdrop and surface. This minimal starter intentionally renders no identity artwork.
10. Run the repository validators and the applicable product tests.

Do not copy visual values out of the upstream stylesheets. Compose with semantic variables only. Do not infer a live action, receipt, status or product event from colour, icon, motion, or a conceptual fixture.
