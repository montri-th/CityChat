CityChat × CityMETER · Landometer

A city data platform built around what your local authority actually needs.

One shared platform, shaped with the first ten municipalities—from the base map to the people who live there.

CityMETER builds the local data foundation: community boundaries, roads, buildings, key places, population data, and the locations municipal teams rely on. CityChat brings that shared picture to frontline officers and people in the community, so everyone can see the same city and act from the same evidence. Each local authority begins with its own priority—not a preset menu.

Local authorities with signed letters of intent · Fiscal Year 2026

Four municipalities. Four priorities. One shared data foundation.

We are working through every requirement raised by the first ten local authorities to learn what a city data platform that truly fits Thai local government should look like. Each municipality starts with the same five core datasets, then builds around its own priorities.

Chon Buri · LOI-513 · May 2026

Saen Suk Town Municipality

home_pinCitywide household locations

Map at least 95% of more than 42,000 households, then connect population, welfare, public health, tax, and public-report data in one map. Initial datasets cover older residents and people with care needs, Smart Safety device users, people with disabilities, pets, and dengue patients.

Si Sa Ket · July 2026

Khun Han Subdistrict Municipality

elderlyVulnerable groups and early warnings

A lesson from the border area: evacuation can be delayed when responders do not know where older people and people with disabilities live. The municipality wants a geolocated vulnerable-groups database, a digital town plan, green-space data, and real-time flood and PM2.5 alerts.

Khon Kaen · LOI-450 · July 2026

Sila Town Municipality

hubCross-department data and community-reported risks

Break down data silos with a shared city data standard and APIs. A public-safety dashboard brings together incident reports and disaster-risk locations identified with residents, covering 100% of the municipality, while making data available to the public.

Nakhon Sawan · August 2026

Ban Dan Subdistrict Municipality

storefrontTourism and the local economy

Build a business database covering at least 80% of the municipality—shops, restaurants, accommodation, and OTOP businesses—alongside visitor information for Khao No–Khao Kaeo: routes, viewpoints, parking, toilets, and safety alerts. Support Thai and English, with links to the municipal website, Facebook page, and LINE Official Account.

Capture conditions on the street. Keep the city register current.

CityScan is CityMETER’s field-survey workflow. As teams move through an area, it records the location and condition of city assets, then feeds that evidence into the same register officers use above—so local decisions do not stop at the date of the last data export.

One city, two perspectives

Municipal staff do not simply need more data; they need data that helps them get work done. Residents do not only need a reporting channel; they need to see that their voice was heard and put to use. CityChat is designed for both groups, working from the same evidence.

Residents · volunteers · community leaders

Understand your city better

  • visibilitySee what is around your home and how the municipality understands the area.
  • translateFollow one issue in plain language, including its source and what the evidence cannot yet tell us.
  • record_voice_overAnswer one question, point out what is missing, or invite someone with local knowledge to confirm it.
  • notifications_activeKnow which issue your response was attached to, and see when something important changes.

Mayors · municipal clerks · department heads · local government staff

Evidence for decisions—and action

  • radarSee which areas are showing which signals, how strong the evidence is, and who to ask next.
  • folder_managedTurn data scattered across files, departments, and LINE groups into places, tasks, owners, and trackable statuses.
  • task_altStart with a system-prepared draft, then have an authorised officer verify it before use.
  • replyReport back to residents using the same evidence that informed the decision.

Five things CityChat is designed to help with

See → Understand → Help answer → Move the work forward → See what changed and retain the learning. Every step begins with a real place and traceable evidence.

01

location_onSee the real place

Know which area or issue you are looking at. Never begin with an empty map or a system menu.

02

fact_checkUnderstand the evidence

See where the signal came from, what it is being compared with, and its limitations.

03

forumHelp answer

Follow up, confirm, report, or invite someone with local knowledge—all through one focused question.

04

assignment_turned_inMove the work forward

Turn information into a monitored area, an inspection task, or a report, according to each officer’s permissions.

05

historySee the outcome. Keep the learning.

Every contribution is acknowledged. Status becomes visible when the task owner confirms it, and each lesson feeds back into the local data foundation.

The city’s data and the community’s voice, side by side

One place can tell one coherent story: what signals are present, what the evidence does and does not show, what to ask next, and one practical action to take. Community voices sit alongside that story—clearly separated from official data and status, and never treated as less important.
